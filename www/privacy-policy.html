Matching World Kids Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Matching World Kids handles information when children and families use the app.

Information Stored on the Device

The app stores game progress on the device, such as unlocked levels, earned stars, sound settings, and world progress. This information is used only to support gameplay and let players continue where they left off.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may process limited technical information, such as device and app information, to deliver, protect, and measure ads.

The app is configured to request child-directed, non-personalized ads for a family-friendly experience.

Children's Privacy

Matching World Kids is designed for children and families. The app does not ask children to create an account, enter a name, upload photos, send messages, or share direct personal information inside the app.

No Account Required

The app does not require sign-in, profile creation, chat, or location sharing.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect app improvements, policy updates, or legal requirements. The latest version should be made available with the published app listing.
